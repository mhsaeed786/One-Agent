# Deep Healthcare Research Recipe (Cherry)

## Steps
1. Define research question and scope
2. Search regulatory sources (ONC, CMS, HL7)
3. Search technical sources (FHIR spec, SMART on FHIR)
4. Search market sources (industry reports, competitor analysis)
5. Synthesize findings with citations
6. Generate structured report with recommendations

## Input Parameters
- `topic`: Research topic
- `depth`: quick | standard | deep
- `focus_areas`: List of specific areas to focus on
- `output_format`: report | summary | bullet_points

## Output
- Executive summary
- Detailed findings with sources
- Analysis and trends
- Actionable recommendations
